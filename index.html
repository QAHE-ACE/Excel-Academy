Good to know

Questions,
answered.

What is the format of the session, and how long will it be?+

Each session delivered by student trainers will last for 1 hour and will take place on campus. Sessions may include explanations, hands-on activities, discussions, and Q&A opportunities.

Will I have training materials to use during the session?+

Yes, the ACE team will provide training materials for all sessions. Trainers are welcome to adapt and customise these materials to better meet the needs of their audience. They are also encouraged to develop additional content based on participants’ interests, requests, and learning requirements. To support successful delivery, the ACE team will provide guidance and training to help trainers facilitate the sessions effectively.

How much preparation time is required before delivering a session?+

We aim to assign trainers to Excel topics and skills they are already familiar with, minimising the amount of preparation required. Before each session, trainers will have a 20 to 30-minute meeting with an ACE team member to discuss the content and address any questions. We also recommend that trainers spend 1 to 2 hours reviewing the materials and practising their delivery beforehand. After the session, trainers will be asked to complete a short self-reflection form to capture their experience and identify any opportunities for improvement.

What if I have a last-minute change of plans before a session I have committed to deliver?+

We expect trainers to honour their commitments and take responsibility for the sessions they agree to deliver. However, we understand that unforeseen circumstances may occasionally arise. If a trainer is unable to deliver a session, we ask that they inform the ACE team at least three hours in advance whenever possible, so that we have sufficient time to arrange a replacement. In such cases, we will activate our backup plan and assign another trainer to deliver the session.

Will I be delivering a session on my own?+

No. You will be paired with another trainer and will deliver the session together. Trainers will take turns acting as the lead trainer and the supporting trainer throughout the programme. ACE will also be occasionally observing the sessions for quality and training purposes.

How will the content of future sessions be decided?+

While we offer a range of established topics, learners are also encouraged to suggest additional subjects they would like to explore. Trainers are welcome to volunteer to develop and deliver new content, activities, or sessions that align with these requests and support learners’ needs.

What level are the sessions aimed at?+

Our sessions are generally aimed at learners with beginner to intermediate Excel skills. However, we recognise that learning needs can vary. If participants would like to explore more advanced Excel functions as a group, trainers are encouraged to propose and develop additional content, subject to interest and availability.
