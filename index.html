Good to know

Questions,
answered.

What is the format and length of a session?+

Each student-led session lasts 90 minutes and takes place on campus. Sessions can include explanations, hands-on activities, discussions and Q&A.

Will I have materials to use?+

Yes. ACE provides materials for every session. You can refine or customise them for your audience, with guidance from the ACE team.

How much preparation is needed?+

We recommend 1 to 2 hours reviewing the materials and rehearsing. Topics are matched to skills you already know.

Will I deliver a session alone?+

No. You will be paired with another trainer, taking turns as lead and supporting trainer throughout the programme.

What level are sessions aimed at?+

Sessions are designed for participants with beginner to intermediate Excel skills.

What if my plans change at short notice?+

We expect trainers to honour their commitments. If unforeseen circumstances arise, ACE will activate a backup plan and arrange another trainer.

How will future session content be chosen?+

ACE will organise sessions around students’ curricula and learning needs to create the greatest benefit for participants.
